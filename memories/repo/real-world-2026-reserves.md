# Real-World 2026 Geological Reserves / Endowment / Production

> Quick-reference table for the Helios-Ascension economy module. Figures
> aggregated from USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (cycled deposits where
> 2026 entries update 2025), Wikipedia cross-checks, and 2024–2025 industry
> reports. Units: Mt = megatonnes (10⁶ t), kt = kilotonnes (10³ t), t = tonnes.
> "Crustal reserves" = economically extractable at current prices.
> "Total endowment" = total geological endowment (crust + oceans + known
> unconventional pools). Scientific notation used for ≥ 10⁹.

> **Remaining gaps:** Mars atmosphere/crust full breakdown; deeper Copper
> USGS specifics; Lunar He-3 geographic map; antimatter/metamaterials/
> computronium (theoretical or synthetic).

## Notes on methodology

- **Crustal reserves** = economically extractable at current prices with current
  technology (~2024–2026).
- **Total endowment** = total geological endowment (extracted + sub-economic +
  natural concentration in seawater, atmosphere, etc.).
- All values are approximate; USGS figures are reported in commodity-specific
  units (e.g. "contained metal" vs "ore") — all normalized to Mt of element or
  compound as shown.
- Gaps marked **TBD** still need filling; targeted USGS commodity PDFs should
  be the next source rather than wide Wikipedia fetches.
